About filmTCG

filmTCG is a movie trading card game.

Open packs from a large film pool spanning movies, actors, and directors. The mix includes recognizable favorites, canon staples, and deeper cuts from across film.

Tier Breakdown

Base
Deeper catalog pulls and lesser-known finds.
Select
Notable work with broader recognition.
Epic
Major standouts, favorites, and high-regard pulls.
Legendary
Iconic, canon-level, and hardest-to-hit cards.

Rarity

Rarity is not based on popularity alone. filmTCG weighs recognition, acclaim, influence, awards visibility, cult reputation, and overall film significance, so the highest tiers can include both iconic titles and strong taste-driven standouts.
The system leaves room for both broad recognition and more film-specific value.

Collect Your Taste

Cards stay in your collection. Duplicates can be traded for Tickets, and lists plus a Top 4 give you a simple way to organize favorites and share what you are into.

Performance Mode

Reduce foil motion, glow effects, and a bit of the opening overhead for smoother play on slower phones.
Privacy

How filmTCG handles your data.

filmTCG is currently a lightweight web app. Most of your collection state, lists, Top 4, and preferences are stored in your browser on your device.

What is stored

On your device: collection progress, stars, lists, Top 4, and interface preferences.
On the server: requests needed to load card pools, TMDB-backed art, and related gameplay data.
Basic telemetry: page views, a few product actions, and client-side errors may be logged to help improve stability during alpha.
Third-party data: movie, actor, and director metadata and artwork may come from TMDB.

What filmTCG does not currently do

No account system yet.
No built-in cloud save yet.
No sale of personal information.

Important note

If you clear your browser storage, switch devices, or use private browsing, your saved collection data may be lost until cloud save exists.

As the app evolves, this page should be updated to reflect analytics, ads, accounts, or any other new systems.
